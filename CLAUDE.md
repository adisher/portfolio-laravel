# Project instructions for Claude

## Web searches and research

- The owner is based in **Pakistan**. For every search or research answer about a
  product, service, API, model, offer, payment method or platform, first check
  whether it works in Pakistan.
- If it is not available in Pakistan, not launched there yet, region-locked,
  needs a VPN, or does not accept Pakistani cards or phone numbers, say so
  **at the top of the answer**, before any other detail. Name the official
  supported regions if they are known.
- If availability in Pakistan cannot be confirmed, say that plainly at the top
  as well, then carry on with the rest of the information.
- Never suggest getting around a regional restriction (VPN signups, foreign
  phone numbers or cards) as the recommended path. At most, mention that it
  may break the provider's terms.

## Writing style

- No em dashes in any output, docs or content.
- Dates and "current" facts must reflect today's date.
